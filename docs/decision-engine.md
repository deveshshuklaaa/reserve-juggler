# Reserve Juggler: Core Decision Model Specification

## 1. Overview & Objective

The **Reserve Juggler Decision Engine** automates cash allocation between a non-interest-bearing checking account (instant liquidity for client payouts, clearing, and ATM networks) and an interest-bearing yield vault (overnight repo, short-duration Treasury bills, or money market fund simulation).

The objective is to **minimize idle checking cash** (maximizing yield) while **keeping the probability of liquidity shortfall strictly below an acceptable risk threshold**.

> **Important Disclaimer**: This document represents a conceptual specification for the HackForge 2026 hackathon prototype. It operates entirely on simulated transaction streams and is not presented as a mathematically guaranteed or production-certified financial model.

---

## 2. Mathematical & Quantitative Framework

### 2.1. Expected Liquidity Requirement ($L_{req}$)
Over a rolling forecast horizon $H$ (e.g., 60 minutes), the forecasting engine estimates net outflows:

$$\hat{O} = \text{Predicted Outflows}, \quad \hat{I} = \text{Predicted Inflows}$$
$$\text{Expected Net Drain } (\mu_{drain}) = \max(0, \hat{O} - \hat{I})$$

### 2.2. Forecast Uncertainty ($\sigma_{forecast}$)
Forecasts are subject to error. We quantify forecast uncertainty either via standard error of the prediction or quantile regression interval (e.g., 95% confidence interval):

$$\text{Uncertainty Buffer} = z_{\alpha} \times \sigma_{forecast}$$

Where $z_{\alpha}$ is the risk tolerance parameter (e.g., $z = 1.96$ for a 95% one-sided coverage corridor).

### 2.3. Operational Safety Buffer ($B_{safety}$)
A minimum baseline reserve required for unexpected systemic shocks, latency in vault withdrawal, or clearing spikes:

$$B_{safety} = \text{Fixed Baseline} + (\text{Checking Balance} \times \text{Buffer Factor})$$

### 2.4. Recommended Liquid Reserve ($R_{req}$)

$$R_{req} = \text{Expected Net Drain } (\mu_{drain}) + \text{Uncertainty Buffer} + B_{safety}$$

---

## 3. Liquidity Gap & Deployable Cash

Given the bank's active checking account balance $C_t$ at timestamp $t$:

$$\text{Deployable Liquidity } (\Delta_t) = C_t - R_{req}$$

Let:
- $\tau_{sweep}$: Minimum threshold for initiating a sweep to avoid excessive micro-transactions (e.g., $10,000).
- $\epsilon_{band}$: Equilibrium tolerance band around the reserve (e.g., $\pm 5\%$).
- $V_t$: Current accessible balance in the Yield Vault.

---

## 4. Decision Rule Logic

Every decision cycle evaluates $\Delta_t$ against predefined risk corridors:

```text
Expected Liquidity Requirement
+
Forecast Uncertainty
+
Operational Safety Buffer
=
Recommended Liquid Reserve

Then:
Current Checking Balance - Required Reserve = Deployable Liquidity
```

### Decision Matrix:

1. **`SWEEP`** (Excess Liquidity):
   - **Condition**: $C_t > R_{req} \times (1 + \epsilon_{band})$ and $\Delta_t \ge \tau_{sweep}$
   - **Action**: Transfer $\Delta_t$ from Checking to Yield Vault.
   - **Rationale**: Idle cash exceeds safety requirements; capture incremental yield without increasing shortfall risk.

2. **`HOLD`** (Equilibrium Corridor):
   - **Condition**: $|C_t - R_{req}| \le (R_{req} \times \epsilon_{band})$ or $0 < \Delta_t < \tau_{sweep}$
   - **Action**: No capital transfer.
   - **Rationale**: Checking liquidity is balanced within the optimal safety corridor; avoid friction costs.

3. **`WITHDRAW`** (Replenishment Required):
   - **Condition**: $C_t < R_{req}$ and $(R_{req} - C_t) \le V_t$
   - **Action**: Liquidate $|C_t - R_{req}|$ from Yield Vault and credit Checking.
   - **Rationale**: Incoming demand or heightened forecast risk requires checking balance replenishment to prevent reserve breach.

4. **`LIQUIDITY_WARNING`** (Insolvency / Extreme Stress):
   - **Condition**: $C_t + V_t < R_{req}$
   - **Action**: Liquidate 100% of available Yield Vault funds to Checking, trigger urgent telemetry alert.
   - **Rationale**: Consolidated reserves fall short of recommended liquidity threshold under current market or outflow conditions.

---

## 5. Auditability & Explainability

Every decision generated by the engine records an immutable audit record:
- Timestamp of calculation
- Inputs ($C_t$, $V_t$, $\hat{I}$, $\hat{O}$, $\sigma$)
- Parameter values ($z_{\alpha}$, $B_{safety}$)
- Computed $R_{req}$ and $\Delta_t$
- Decision type and deterministic human-readable explanation

This enables full forensic review during simulation runs and post-stress-test analytics.
